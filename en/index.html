Why these apps exist

Each app starts from a concrete everyday need: regaining control over screen time, improving sleep when specialist care is hard to reach, tracking a menstrual cycle privately, or relying on tools that work without connectivity. Existing alternatives often lock the most useful features behind paywalls or fund themselves with user data.

Pilmil Studio is built on a simple principle: people trying to sleep better, understand their body or protect their attention should not have to pay for it or give up their most intimate data.

How they are built

  • Grounded in research

    Every feature rests on published evidence or an explicit design choice, documented with sources on each app's website.

  • Only what belongs in the app

    No streaks, no badges, no retention notifications. Detailed explanations live on the website, not inside the app.

  • AI-assisted & verified

    Part of the code is drafted with AI assistance, then reviewed, tested on real devices and maintained by the team.

  • Direct accountability

    A bug, a privacy concern or a questionable source: write to us or open an issue on GitHub for a direct answer.

Why open source

An app dealing with health, sleep or cycles requires trust. Open code lets anyone verify rather than take our word for it: you can inspect what the app does with your data—nothing.

It also guarantees these tools remain available over time, and keeps the door open for fixes, translations and reviews, especially by healthcare professionals.
